# Painted Hearts — 4.0

The 3.9 render gave Bark protruding spherical eyes and gave both characters strong
facial shadows. This revision uses shallow ink, cream, iris and highlight shapes
that follow the head, reduces pupil travel, and retains deliberate blinks, brows,
smiles and talking. Mark has a quieter swept hair silhouette and a shallow nose.
Bark has fewer raised chips and ridges and a smaller, uncluttered facial panel.

Broad ambient fill and soft directional lights preserve volume in clothing and
wood while face paint remains readable. Specular highlights are disabled on the
characters. Goggles, scarf, satchel, blossom and acorn remain, with a quieter canopy.

Each actor has six preallocated paper flecks (Mark) or petals (Bark), shown briefly
on entering a cheer. They do not affect collisions, rewards or saves. Pausing
freezes their lifetime; reduced motion immediately hides them. Scarf and leaf
motion retain their existing pause and reduced-motion handling.

All original PNG paintings remain intact. Original and articulated painting styles
remain selectable. Game movement, the library discoveries and the minimal HUD
retain their existing behavior. No new stage is included.

Validation is recorded in test_output by the source and Android verification tools.
Physical Android testing remains necessary before claiming device performance.
